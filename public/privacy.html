Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the private KO Reps Invoice Desk application handles information, including data received through Google OAuth.

Information the application stores

Invoice Desk stores information entered by authorized staff, such as client contact and billing details, invoices, payment records, business settings and email delivery status. It also stores the authorization needed to send mail from the connected company Gmail account.

Google user data

Invoice Desk requests the Gmail permission gmail.send. This permission is used only to send an invoice or approved client message when an authorized staff member initiates that action. The application does not request permission to read Gmail messages, contacts, drafts or sent mail.

Google OAuth credentials are stored on the private application server and are used only to provide this email-sending function. Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with data brokers. It is disclosed to service providers only as needed to operate the application, such as the hosting and email services used to deliver an authorized message.

Protection and retention

Access to business records is password protected. The Gmail refresh credential is encrypted before storage. Records are retained for business, accounting and legal purposes. Authorization can be removed by disconnecting Gmail in Invoice Desk or revoking the application's access in the connected Google Account.

Deletion and questions

An authorized KO Reps representative may request deletion of the stored Gmail authorization or other personal information, subject to accounting and legal retention requirements, by emailing robbie@ecopromise.com.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the application or its data practices change. The effective date at the top will be updated at that time.

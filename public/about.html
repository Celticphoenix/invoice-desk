Private business tool

Simple invoicing for KO Reps.

Invoice Desk is an internal application used by authorized KO Reps staff to create invoices, send them to clients, provide secure Stripe payment links, and prepare records for the company accountant.

Access is private. The application, client list, invoices and financial records require the company password. This public page contains no client or business data.

How Gmail is used

When an authorized staff member connects the company Gmail account, Invoice Desk requests permission to send email only. It uses that permission to send an invoice or approved client message after a staff member explicitly asks it to do so.

  • Invoice Desk does not read the Gmail inbox.
  • It does not read contacts, drafts or sent messages.
  • It does not send email without an authorized user initiating the action.

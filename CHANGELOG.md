# Changelog

## 1.2.0 — 2026-09-14

- Add selected-client email campaigns sent as individual Gmail messages.
- Add separate express, implied, needs-review, and unsubscribed client marketing states.
- Block missing, expired, unreviewed, and unsubscribed recipients at save and send time.
- Add business identity, mailing address, contact details, and unsubscribe instructions to every campaign.
- Add a 50-recipient safety limit, message preview, per-recipient results, and duplicate-safe retries.
- Keep open tracking, bulk BCC, bounce processing, and automated web unsubscribe out of scope.
- Expand automated coverage to 20 tests, including permission rechecks and a duplicate-safe end-to-end campaign send.

## 1.1.0 — 2026-09-14

- Add a reusable services and pricing catalogue.
- Add client editing, optional contact details, and private notes.
- Add safe invoice duplication and percentage-fee shortcuts.
- Add monthly accountant packages with revenue-by-category reporting.
- Add duplicate-safe FreshBooks client and item importing.
- Add optional Gmail send-only delivery with encrypted authorization, previews, retries, PDF attachments, and duplicate-send protection.
- Add Interac e-Transfer as an optional payment method alongside PayPal and Stripe.
- Expand automated coverage to 16 tests.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-14

- Initial open-source release.

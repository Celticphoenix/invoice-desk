# Comparison graphic notes

The social graphic compares three posted FreshBooks Canada plans with Invoice Desk. It deliberately covers a small set of invoicing essentials instead of implying that Invoice Desk replaces FreshBooks' broader accounting platform.

FreshBooks plan details and regular monthly prices were checked on September 14, 2026 at <https://www.freshbooks.com/en-ca/pricing>. Temporary promotional prices were excluded because they change.

FreshBooks documents marketing workflows through integrations such as HubSpot at <https://support.freshbooks.com/hc/en-us/articles/360028927111-How-does-the-Hubspot-app-work>. The comparison therefore says “Via integration” rather than claiming FreshBooks cannot support marketing. A native selected-client campaign tool is not listed in the FreshBooks plan table; that wording is an inference from the official plan table and support documentation, not a claim about every third-party integration.

Invoice Desk claims are backed by the repository and automated tests. “Unlimited campaigns” means the app does not limit how many campaign records can be created; each send is limited to 50 eligible recipients and remains subject to Gmail sending limits. Its campaign feature is intentionally small: recorded permission states, separate Gmail messages, per-recipient results, and unsubscribe instructions. It does not include bounce processing, analytics, open tracking, or automated web unsubscribe. The graphic expressly states that Invoice Desk does not include expense tracking and is not full accounting software.
